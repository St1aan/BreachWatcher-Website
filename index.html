Cybersecurity Tips

Expert guidance to
keep you safe online

Our goal is not only to protect your online identity but to educate and build a safer community for everyone.

🎣
Social Engineering
What is phishing, smishing, vishing, and quishing?
Fraudulent emails, SMS, phone calls, and QR codes designed to trick you. Learn to spot each attack type before you become a victim.
🔓
Vulnerabilities
What are zero-days and software vulnerabilities?
Zero-days are unpatched flaws unknown to vendors — making them extremely dangerous when actively exploited by attackers in the wild.
🔑
Passwords & Auth
Why you should use a password manager
Password reuse is one of the biggest security risks. A manager generates strong, unique passwords for every account — even if one is breached, the rest stay safe.
🛡️
Authentication
What is multi-factor authentication (MFA)?
SMS OTP, authenticator apps, hardware keys — not all MFA is equal. Learn which method actually keeps attackers out and which can be bypassed.
🔐
Authentication
Passkeys explained — the future of logging in
Passkeys replace passwords with cryptographic key pairs. They can't be phished, stolen in a breach, or reused — and major platforms support them today.
💾
Data Breaches
What happens to your data after a breach?
Stolen credentials don't disappear — they're sold on dark web forums, recycled into combo lists, and used in credential stuffing attacks for years.
🤖
Data Breaches
What is credential stuffing?
Attackers use leaked username and password pairs to automate logins across hundreds of sites. Password reuse is what makes this attack so devastatingly effective.
🔒
Malware
What is ransomware and how does it spread?
Ransomware encrypts your files and demands payment. It spreads through phishing emails, pirated software, and exposed remote desktop services.
🦠
Malware
What is an infostealer?
Infostealers silently harvest your saved browser passwords, cookies, and session tokens — often bypassing MFA entirely by stealing active sessions.
📶
Network Safety
Public Wi-Fi risks and when to use a VPN
Public Wi-Fi exposes you to man-in-the-middle attacks and evil twin hotspots. Learn when a VPN actually helps — and when it doesn't.
🌐
Safe Behaviour
How to spot a fake website
A padlock doesn't mean a site is safe. Learn to spot lookalike domains, visual clones, and QR code redirects before you hand over your credentials.
👁️
Safe Behaviour
Social media oversharing and OSINT
Attackers don't need to hack you — your public profiles often reveal enough to craft convincing targeted attacks or answer your security questions.
📱
Mobile Security
Why you should check app permissions
Apps requesting SMS access can intercept your OTP codes, bypassing MFA. Learn how to audit permissions on Android and iOS and spot red flags.
🚨
Account Security
What is account takeover (ATO)?
Account takeover connects every threat together — from breach to credential stuffing to full account loss. Learn the signs, the lifecycle, and how to recover.
